# OctoAcme Project Management

Welcome to OctoAcme's centralized project management documentation. This folder contains comprehensive guides and templates for running structured, iterative projects across cross-functional teams.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative project management process inspired by industry best practices and tailored for cross-functional software teams. 

**Core Principles:**
- **Customer-first:** Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative delivery:** Deliver small, testable increments continuously
- **Clear ownership:** Each project has clearly defined roles and responsibilities
- **Data-informed decisions:** Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Transparency & Psychological Safety:** Encourage feedback, learning, and open communication
- **Continuous Improvement:** Regularly reflect, adapt, and refine processes

### Typical Project Lifecycle

The standard project flow consists of five key phases:

1. **Initiation:** Problem definition, stakeholder alignment, and goal setting
2. **Planning:** Breaking work into deliverable increments, prioritizing, estimating scope, and identifying risks
3. **Execution & Tracking:** Iterative delivery, regular demos, progress tracking, risk escalation, and quality checks
4. **Release & Deployment:** Release-readiness checklists, deployment best practices, and incident playbooks
5. **Retrospective & Improvement:** Post-milestone reviews, action item tracking, and process documentation updates

### Core Roles

Each project is staffed with clearly defined roles:

- **Project Manager (PM):** Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Manager (PdM):** Defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success
- **Developers:** Implement features, collaborate on design, and ensure quality
- **QA/Testing:** Validate quality and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders:** Provide inputs, approvals, and strategic guidance

## Key Artifacts You'll Create

Across a typical project, your team will produce:

- **Project Charter / One-pager** — Captures problem, goals, success metrics, stakeholders
- **Roadmap & Release Plan** — High-level timeline and deliverables
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** — Prioritized work with acceptance criteria
- **Risk Register** — Identifies, assesses, and tracks risks
- **Retrospective Notes & Action Items** — Captures learnings and improvements

## Communication Cadence

Effective communication keeps teams aligned:

- **Daily Standups** — 15 minutes; focus on progress, blockers, dependencies
- **Weekly PM + PdM Sync** — Alignment on priorities, risks, and decisions
- **Twice-Weekly Team Syncs** — Delivery updates and blocker resolution
- **Demo/Review Sessions** — End of sprint or milestone showcases
- **Monthly Stakeholder Updates** — High-level progress and business impact
- **Retrospectives** — After each sprint, release, or major milestone
