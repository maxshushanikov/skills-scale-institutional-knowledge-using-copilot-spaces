### Summary of New Content

Identify and add additional personas/roles to the project management documentation to enhance clarity, accountability, and effective collaboration. New roles will include descriptions of responsibilities and their interactions with existing roles.

### Why is this update needed?

Expanding defined personas addresses gaps encountered in real projects where certain responsibilities fall outside current descriptions. Adding roles such as UX Designer, Business Analyst, Scrum Master, and Technical Writer ensures onboarding clarity, reduces single-person dependency risk, and aligns the documentation with industry standards. Clearly establishing how these new roles work with PM, PdM, and developers will clarify handoffs and expectations, leading to more predictable project outcomes and faster ramp-up for new teammates.

### Suggested Content (optional)

Potential new personas to add:

---

### UX Designer
- Crafts and validates user flows, mockups, and prototypes
- Collaborates with Product Manager and developers to ensure features deliver customer value
- Advocates for usability and accessibility best practices
- Gathers and incorporates user feedback
- Works with QA for acceptance criteria based on UX/UI

**Interactions:**
- Works closely with PdM on requirements
- Collaborates with Developers for implementation
- Reports UX findings and risks to PM

---

### Business Analyst
- Refines requirements from business stakeholders into actionable items
- Maintains alignment between business objectives and technical delivery
- Updates and clarifies success criteria with stakeholders and PM
- Documents process maps and impact analyses

**Interactions:**
- Liaises between stakeholders, Product, and Developers
- Assists PM with risk analysis and key communications

---

### Scrum Master
- Coaches team on agile values and practical ceremonies
- Facilitates standups, retrospectives, sprint planning
- Shields the team from interruptions and removes blockers
- Tracks process improvement action items

**Interactions:**
- Partners with PM on delivery and process health
- Coordinates with all roles to address impediments

---

### Technical Writer
- Documents process changes, onboarding guides, and key artifacts
- Ensures knowledge is up to date and accessible
- Works with all roles to clarify documentation needs
- Maintains process doc updates in the repo

**Interactions:**
- Consults with PMs, PdMs, Developers, QA
- Publishes updates after stakeholder review
